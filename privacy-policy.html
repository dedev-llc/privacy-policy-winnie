1. Overview

Winnie the Pooh is a story listening app that lets you browse chapters, listen to narrated stories, save progress, customize playback preferences, and optionally download stories for offline listening.

We designed the App to collect only the information needed to provide these features. You may use the App as a guest or create an account with email and password or Google Sign-In.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo URL (when signing in with Google), and a unique user identifier from Firebase Authentication.
  • Guest session data: if you continue as a guest, Firebase may create an anonymous account so your listening progress can be saved and later linked if you register.
  • App preferences: narration speed, autoplay settings, read-along highlight preference, and selected language.
  • Listening progress: chapter progress, playback position, and related story activity synced with our backend when you are signed in or using a linked guest session.
  • Downloaded content metadata: information about stories saved on your device for offline use. Downloaded audio and files remain stored locally on your device.
  • Support and password recovery data: email address and one-time verification codes if you request password reset.
  • Device and app data: basic technical information needed for notifications, background audio playback, download tasks, network connectivity checks, and app performance (such as operating system version and app version).
We do not intentionally collect precise location data, contacts, photos from your camera roll, microphone recordings, or payment card details through the App.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and manage your account or guest session;
  • authenticate you and keep your session secure;
  • deliver story content, narration, search, and chapter details;
  • save and restore listening progress across devices;
  • remember your language and playback preferences;
  • enable offline downloads and background listening;
  • send app-related notifications where permitted by your device settings;
  • help with account recovery and customer support;
  • maintain, protect, and improve the App and our services.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the App, and only as needed to provide our services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, secure sign-in, and related identity services.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in using your Google account.
  • Our backend API — hosted services used to store preferences, progress, and deliver app content.
  • Cloud and infrastructure providers — hosting, storage, content delivery, and text-to-speech/narration services used to generate or deliver audio content.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. You can review Google’s policies at policies.google.com/privacy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users and our rights, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

5. Storage and retention

Account, preference, and progress data may be stored on secure servers operated by us or our service providers. Some data is also stored locally on your device — for example cached images, downloaded stories, and settings — using device storage mechanisms such as SharedPreferences.

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. We may retain certain records for longer where required for legal, security, or backup purposes. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your account and associated data;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

You can update language and playback preferences inside the App. You can disable notifications in your device settings. You can delete downloaded stories from within the App. To delete your account or exercise privacy rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for families and story listeners of all ages, but account creation and certain features may require parental guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections where supported and industry-standard authentication practices through Firebase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

If you use the App from outside the country where our servers or service providers operate, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries that may have different data protection laws than your own.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice in the App or by other reasonable means. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
