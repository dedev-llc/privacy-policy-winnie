Winnie the Pooh · Account Deletion

Delete your account & data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Winnie the Pooh account and associated data for the mobile app published by DE DEV LLC (package ID: com.winnie.app).

App: Winnie the Pooh Developer: DE DEV LLC Last updated: September 1, 2026

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing us. We will verify your request and process it within 7–14 business days.

  1. Send an email to privacy@dedev-llc.com from the email address linked to your Winnie the Pooh account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request – Winnie the Pooh.
  3. In the message, include the email address you used to sign up (if different from the sender address) and confirm that you want your account permanently deleted.
  4. We will confirm receipt by email and notify you when deletion is complete.
Email privacy@dedev-llc.com

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove or anonymize the following:

  • Account credentials and profile information (name, email, user ID)
  • Google Sign-In linkage associated with your account
  • App preferences (language, narration speed, autoplay, read-along settings)
  • Listening progress and chapter playback history synced to our servers
  • Guest-session data linked to your account (if applicable)

Data that may be kept

Data type Why it may be kept
Security, fraud-prevention, and server logs Retained for up to 90 days where required for security, troubleshooting, or legal compliance, then deleted or anonymized.
Backup copies May remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being permanently removed.
Records required by law Retained only as long as legally required (e.g., billing or tax records if you had a paid subscription).

Data stored on your device

Some data is stored locally on your phone and is not automatically removed when we delete your server account:

  • Downloaded stories for offline listening
  • Cached images and narration audio files
  • Local app settings stored on the device

To remove this data, delete downloaded stories in the app (Profile → Downloaded Stories) and uninstall the app from your device.

Guest accounts

If you used the app in guest mode without creating a full account, contact us at privacy@dedev-llc.com with any details you can provide (device, approximate usage date). We will make reasonable efforts to delete associated guest session data.

More information

For full details on how we collect and use data, see our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to saved progress, preferences, and any premium benefits tied to your account.
